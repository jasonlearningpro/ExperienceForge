Group cards in boxes

Draw a labeled box around cards on the Flow Canvas to show which belong together, such as "Step 1: Start the course" or "Safety quiz". A box is only a drawing for you: it changes nothing in the course and is not in the published course.

  1. Choose ▭ Add group at the bottom of the canvas.
  2. For a rectangle, press and drag diagonally across the canvas. For any other shape, click each corner one by one. It can be any shape, as long as you click its corners one by one. Click the first corner again, or press Enter, to close it. Backspace takes back the last corner and Esc cancels.
  1. Type the label. It shows in a tab at the top of the box. Pick a Color if you like.

To box cards you have already selected, select them and choose Group selected cards. This is also the way to make a group with the keyboard alone.

Work with a group.

  • Let go of it. Click anywhere else, or press Esc, and the group's handles go away.
  • Move it. Drag its label or its edge. The cards inside it move with it. A card is inside when its middle is inside the shape. Undo puts everything back in one step.
  • Resize it. Select the group, then drag a square handle on the dashed frame round it. The whole shape stretches from the opposite side, and the cards stay where they are.
  • Change its corners. Select the group, then drag a round corner handle.
  • Wrap it again. Fit to cards redraws the box around the cards now inside it.
  • Remove it. Delete group removes only the box. The cards stay.
  • Let go of it. Click a card or empty canvas, or press Esc.
  • Add a card. Drag a card over the box. While it is over the right place, the box turns stronger with a dashed edge and says "Drop here to add to this group".
  • With the keyboard. Press Tab to reach a group. The arrow keys move it with its cards (hold Shift for bigger steps), Enter edits the label, and Delete removes it.

Tidy up keeps your groups. When you tidy up the canvas, the cards are arranged as usual, then each box is drawn again around the cards it held. A shape you drew by hand becomes an outline with room around those cards.

Good to know

  • A course can have up to 100 groups.
  • A group with no cards inside stays where you drew it.
