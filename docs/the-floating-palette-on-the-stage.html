The floating palette on the stage

Select anything on the stage (text, a button, a picture, a shape) and the item toolbar, a small palette that stays in one place, lights up with tools for that kind of item: text style and size, colors (theme colors first), alignment on the screen, ✦ Animate…, layer order, lock, hide, duplicate and delete. The palette does not move when you pick a different item. It sits across the top of the stage, just under the Add toolbar. Drag its ⋮⋮ grip (or any empty part of the palette) to put it somewhere else (it stays there), or focus the grip and press the arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps); double-click the grip to put it back at its first place. Select an audio layer to get its own palette: listen, volume, + Caption at playhead, ✨ Auto-transcribe.
