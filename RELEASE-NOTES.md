# Earlier release notes

The notes for versions before the current one. The app was called KnowledgeFoundry until version 0.8.0, when it became ExperienceForge, so these notes use the old name. The notes for 0.7.0, 0.7.2 and 0.7.5 were not kept.

## 0.7.6 (everything since 0.7.4)

- **Buttons, videos and pop-ups on quiz pages.** Add a button or a video to a question, results or introduction page, then use triggers to open a pop-up or panel, show or hide an item, or play a video or sound. A bar above the stage switches between the page and each pop-up while you design it. The player's own Submit, Next and Try again buttons still work, so a learner can answer without opening anything.
- **Words, pictures and shapes on quiz pages.** Put a logo, a panel behind the question or a note on any quiz page, in front of or behind the quiz items.
- **Much more styling for quiz page items.** Every item has Font, Corners, Border and Shadow, and items that are only words can have a panel behind them.
- **Zoom the editing stage** with the zoom box in the stage bar, Ctrl+plus, Ctrl+minus, Ctrl+0, or Ctrl with the mouse wheel. It works on screens, pop-ups and quiz pages.
- **Four new color schemes for the editor:** Foundry Warm Slate, Blueprint Navy, Studio Parchment and Synth-Chroma. Pick one in the Appearance list at the bottom right of the window or under View → Appearance.
- **Shadow distances are now labeled Horizontal and Vertical,** with help text on which way a positive or negative number moves the shadow.
- **The example courses show the new features.** Each one has a button on its quiz pages that opens a pop-up or panel: a hint in Safety First, the call video in Handling a Difficult Call, and a "Why do we ask?" panel in Your First Week.
- Check course checks the new quiz page items the same way it checks a screen, and says which page a problem is on.
- Fixed: Check course said a picture used only on a quiz page wasn't used, even though it was exported with the course.

## 0.7.4

- **Quiz pages are designed on the stage.** The question page, results page and introduction page of a quiz are laid out like any other screen. A quiz node can have its own design. Start from one of 13 built-in layouts or save your own as a template.
- **Mix the answers again on Try again** is a new option for each question.
- **Shadows** for text, pictures and shapes: color, distance, blur and opacity. The controls are in Properties and in the floating tools on the stage.
- **Light, Dark and System** for the editor itself.
- **Hotspots on a phone:** a picture can keep its markers in place instead of stacking them in a list.
- **The four player button shapes now look different.** Ghost is the bare icon with no background.
- **The user guide has pictures on every page,** in the app and online at https://jasonlearningpro.github.io/ExperienceForge/
- Check course now also warns about text that is hard to read against the shape behind it.
- Fixed: items piling up on top of each other on a phone when the pointer moved over them.
- Fixed: the pop-ups in the Safety First example were blank.
- Fixed: Check course findings and the Layers panel can be used with the keyboard, and dialogs keep Tab inside them.
- Fixed: a selected video's heading said Image.
- The Assets tab has an icon on each import button, and tooltips across the app are shorter and more consistent.

Republish your courses to get the player fixes.
